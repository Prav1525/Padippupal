## Findings and changes made

- **Chapter coverage:** All 12 bank chapter names match the official Class 6 chapter sequence.
- **Chapter 1 — scientific method:** Questions on observation, testable questions, changing one variable and recording observations fit the chapter's investigation approach.
- **Chapter 4 — magnets:** The answers about magnetic materials, poles, compass direction and attraction/repulsion agree with the chapter. The chapter also explicitly warns against heating, dropping or hammering magnets and keeping them near mobile phones or remote controls.
- **Chapter 6 — materials:** Replaced the float-versus-solubility question with an original transparent-container scenario. The chapter explicitly teaches transparent/translucent/opaque materials and solubility; the revised question is more tightly tied to that chapter's stated content.
- **Chapter 11 — natural resources:** The renewable/non-renewable distinction and examples are consistent with the chapter's definitions and summary.
- **Chapter 12 — constellations:** Tightened the model answer. Rather than defining a constellation only as a pattern, it now says a constellation is a defined region of the sky that often includes a recognisable star pattern. This preserves the distinction made in the current textbook.
- **Question labels:** All 60 remain labelled as original syllabus-aligned practice items (48 in that style and 12 chapter-context items in the full bank). They are **not** represented as verified verbatim book-back questions.
- **Exam pattern:** The project specification now states plainly that the Tamil Nadu school-paper format is provisional until a cited, verified past-paper collection is assembled.
